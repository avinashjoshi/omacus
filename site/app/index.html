Private by design

          
Pages stay in this browser, on this device. With sync on, a sealed copy can go to your other phones and computers. A share link carries the page after the #, which browsers don't send to any server. Export a copy from Settings. On iPhone and iPad, add Omacus to the Home Screen so Safari keeps them.

          
This browser may clear saved pages. Export a copy from Settings to keep one.
