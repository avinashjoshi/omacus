Omacus is a free notepad calculator for Linux, macOS and Windows. Because money is just another unit to it, you can add, split and convert prices in the same line, and keep a whole trip's or invoice's worth of math on one page.

Symbols or codes

Use the symbol you'd write by hand, $ £ € ¥ ₹ ₩ ₽ ₺, or any three-letter currency code, before or after the number: $30, 30 USD and USD 30 are the same. Thousands separators like $1,299 or ₹1,50,000 are fine, and k means thousands.

Convert with in or to. Results in dollars are shown as $; other currencies keep their code so it's always clear which one you're looking at.

Everyday money math

Multiply, split, take percentages and work with rates like dollars per hour. Omacus keeps track of the units, so $85/hour * 160 hours is money, not hours.

Rates, and working offline

Omacus downloads exchange rates for 160+ currencies from a free public source when it starts, and saves them on your computer. They're refreshed every 12 hours while you're online. On a plane or without Wi-Fi, currency math keeps working with the last rates it saved. Nothing about you or your pages is sent anywhere; the request only asks for the latest rates.

Questions

How many currencies does Omacus support?

More than 160, covering every currency with a published exchange rate from its rate source, from USD, EUR and JPY to smaller ones like NPR and KES.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

From open.er-api.com, a free exchange rate service. They're cached on your computer and refreshed every 12 hours.

Does currency conversion work offline?

Yes, using the last rates that were saved. You only need a connection to refresh them.

Can I add amounts in different currencies?

Yes. €50 + £20 in USD converts both and adds them. Without in, the answer is in the first currency on the line.

Does it support crypto like Bitcoin?

Not yet. Only government currencies are included for now.
