Privacy

Omacus has no ads and no analytics. Desktop pages stay on this computer. The web app keeps pages in the browser until you turn on sync. This page lists what is sent, and why.

The desktop app

  • Your pages are plain text files in a folder on your computer (see where files live). With sync on, a sealed copy can leave this computer. The server cannot read it. The Turn on sync button in the sidebar explains sync, then sets it up in that window. This site does not collect an email.
  • Exchange rates. Omacus downloads the latest rates from open.er-api.com at most every 12 hours while you use it, and keeps them on disk so currency math works offline. The request carries nothing from your pages.
  • Checking for updates. Omacus asks GitHub for the latest version number when it starts (at most once an hour) and every 4 hours while it's open. Put check_for_updates = false in settings.toml to turn that off. A new version is downloaded, from GitHub, only when you choose Update now or run omacus update.
  • Nothing else. No telemetry, no crash reports, no usage statistics.

The web app

  • Your pages are saved in your browser until you turn on sync. Sync sends a sealed copy. The server cannot read the pages, the names, or the recovery words. A sealed copy is also saved each night and kept for about 30 days. That copy is not opened. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes the local copy, so export a copy (⋯ menu) if the pages matter.
  • Exchange rates come from the same source as the desktop app, kept in the browser for 12 hours.
  • The app itself is downloaded from omacus.com and cached so it works offline.

Share links

A share link carries the page inside it, compressed into the part after the #. Browsers never send that part to a server, so omacus.com doesn't receive your page when someone opens the link.

Anyone who has the link can read the page, so treat it like the page itself. Chat apps that show link previews may store the whole link on their servers.

This website

omacus.com is static pages, hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. Like any host, they handle your IP address and a log of requests to serve the pages. Cloudflare counts visits without cookies. There are no cookies, ads or third-party trackers, and the fonts are served from omacus.com itself.

Terms

Omacus is free and open source under the MIT license, which is the whole agreement: use it for anything, and it comes as is, without warranty. Double-check numbers that matter, such as taxes, invoices and medicine doses, and remember that exchange rates are a day's mid-market rate, not what your bank charges.

Changes and questions

If what Omacus sends ever changes, this page changes first; its history is public in the source repository. Questions are welcome as a GitHub issue.
